# Product spec (summary)

Source: *The Omni Operating System — Chair and CEO Briefing (v4)*, with the clarifications in the v4 product update. Principle: **Begin with the buyer. Focus on less. Execute every week.**

**Users.** Portfolio company leadership teams (CEO, executives, Chair/Board observers, company administrators) run their business in Omni OS; Omni's investment and operating team monitors execution across their assigned companies. Omni OS replaces Ninety.io entirely; there is no integration with it. It does not replace accounting, HRIS or CRM systems.

**Four pillars.**
- **North Star** — where are we going? Vision → Mission → Values (always in that order), target end-state, strategic positioning, market opportunity, financial ambition, revenue/EBITDA targets, exit equity story, potential buyers, what makes the company strategically valuable, and 3–5 conditions for success. Versioned.
- **Flight Plan** — how do we get there? North Star → Three-Year Outcomes → 12-Month Objectives → 90-Day Priorities. About three priorities a quarter, each with one owner, a defined outcome, measurable success criteria, milestones, a deadline, an on track / at risk / off track status, a link to its objective and KPIs where relevant.
- **Crew** — who will deliver it? Organisation structure, ELT, functional accountabilities, ownership, and *agreed organisational actions only* (critical roles, approved recruitment priorities, structure and dependencies, capability initiatives). No individual assessments of any kind.
- **Mission Control** — how do we ensure execution? Priorities → Owners → Scorecard → Issues → Decisions → Actions, answering "what needs attention this week?". *Rhythm* is the meeting and reporting cadence inside Mission Control.

**Blueprint.** The company dashboard, readable in 30 seconds: what needs attention this week (separated from routine on-track activity), North Star, Three-Year Outcomes, scoreboard, 90-Day Priorities and 12-Month Objectives, Crew summary, Mission Control and Next Steps. Uses the company's branding.

**Monday KPI scorecard.** KPI | Actual | Target | Status | Agreed corrective action — nothing else, no commentary. Corrective actions only against off-track KPIs (from actions linked to the KPI, with owner and deadline); "Action required — no corrective action agreed." where there is none; at-risk KPIs show status only; missing results are "not reported". Generated automatically → approved by the CEO → sent to the selected Omni recipients (email + in-app). History kept. Visible to company leadership and Omni only.

**Weekly ELT meeting.** Four parts: Scorecard (exceptions only) → 90-day priorities (concise update and evidence) → Issues → Actions (decisions and actions, each with one owner and a deadline). Everything is updated in the meeting; open items carry over; history is permanent and searchable.

**Reporting** — three distinct outputs, separate from the Monday scorecard: the CFO's monthly flash (within 5 working days), the monthly one-page Board check-in (papers 3 working days before the Board, accounts within 21 days) and the quarterly Board pack. Figures come from the scorecard and plan; management adds commentary and publishes, which freezes the figures.

**Quarterly offsite (stop and reset)** — 8 stages: revisit the North Star; review the last 90 days (achieved / partly achieved / missed / stopped, with rationale; carry forward or stop); review the scorecard (keep or drop measures); employee listening; customer listening; Crew; 2–3 strategic issues; set the next ~3 priorities. Approval activates the new plan; previous plans remain comparable. The annual strategy reset (North Star → Flight Plan → 12-month objectives → budget) is an offsite flagged as annual. The CEO prepares a company-wide update afterwards.

**Cycles.** Employee and customer listening at least twice a year (aggregate results and themes only; actions tracked). First 100 days: the 7-step operating cycle and timetable as a lightweight tracker (Mission Control set-up from week 1; branding optional). Calendar & governance: Boards, offsites, reporting deadlines, listening cycles and investor requirements against a configurable financial year — no hard-coded consent thresholds.

**Portfolio (Omni).** Always Omni-branded. Monday scorecards first (off-track KPIs and their corrective actions), then per company: North Star progress, current priorities and delivery status, KPI exceptions, matters needing Omni support, overdue commitments, organisational actions, reporting compliance, last/next offsite.

**Branding.** Omni's official identity (logo and palette from the deck, Rubik) by default. Each company can set its own logo and primary/secondary/accent colours in Settings → Appearance & Branding with a live preview; shades, hovers and readable text colours are derived automatically; statuses keep fixed semantic colours. Stored per company; only the CEO, company admins and Omni can change it.

**Security.** Database-enforced tenant isolation and role permissions, Omni-only confidential data, audit trail, versioned North Star.
