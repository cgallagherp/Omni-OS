# Product spec (summary)

**Users.** Portfolio company leadership teams run their business in Omni OS; Omni's investment and operating teams monitor execution across their assigned companies.

**Pillars → modules.** North Star (destination) · Flight Plan (Winning Moves → Annual Goals → Quarterly Priorities → Actions) · Crew (accountability) · Rhythm (weekly/monthly/quarterly cadence). They are views of one operating plan.

**The connected chain (the core requirement).** A KPI deteriorates (Scoreboard) → it is flagged on the Blueprint "Needs attention" strip and becomes a KPI exception on the next weekly agenda → in the live meeting an issue is raised from it → a decision is recorded and an action assigned to an owner with a deadline → the action appears in the following meeting's "Previous actions" until done → Omni sees the deterioration, the exception and any escalation on the Portfolio. Every record links up the chain to the North Star (breadcrumb on detail pages) and down to KPIs, issues, decisions and actions.

**Key screens.** Blueprint (30-second read: health strip, needs attention, North Star + Winning Moves, scoreboard, 90-day commitments, annual goals, issues, decisions, execution & rhythm); Scoreboard grid with inline entry; live meeting interface for a boardroom laptop; Portfolio overview and comparison.

**KPI status.** Higher-is-better, lower-is-better or within-range, with a per-metric at-risk tolerance band; targets can be phased per period (budgets). Exceptions = off track, at risk, deteriorating, or not reported for the last completed period.

**Meetings.** Agenda generated from live data at start and snapshotted; record attendance, review KPIs (update values in-meeting), update priority status/progress, raise/discuss/resolve issues, record decisions, assign actions, notes; finish with an auto-drafted summary and rating; unresolved issues and open actions carry forward; next weekly is scheduled automatically.

**Security.** Database-enforced tenant isolation, role-based permissions, Omni-only confidential data, audit trail, versioned North Star.

**AI (phase 2).** Assistant grounded in the same snapshot with source references and facts/recommendations separated.
