| 20 | Brand colours never replace semantic status colours. | 23 | Where the deck describes the Monday CEO email as covering exceptions, decisions, people and M&A, the product follows the v4 update: a KPI scorecard only (KPI, Actual, Target, Status, Agreed corrective action) with no commentary. | The update explicitly overrides the deck on this point. |
| 24 | Recorded KPI values are treated as the approved actuals; the scorecard is rebuilt server-side at approval and frozen in `weekly_scorecards.rows`. Published Board reports freeze their figures in `board_reports.snapshot`. | Avoids a separate value-approval workflow while keeping what was sent immutable. |
| 25 | Email delivery uses Resend when `RESEND_API_KEY`/`EMAIL_FROM` are set; otherwise sends are recorded with status `not_configured` and recipients see the scorecard in-app. No email provider is configured in this environment, so email sending has not been exercised. | No SMTP/provider credentials available; nothing pretends to send. |
| 26 | Monday scorecard recipients are the Omni members of the company, selectable at approval. Observers (Chair/Board) cannot see scorecards. | "Access limited to recipients and company leadership." |
| 27 | Report deadlines: flash = 5 working days after month end; Board papers = 3 working days before the next scheduled Board, falling back to the 21-day accounts deadline when no Board is scheduled. Bank holidays are not modelled. | Deck rules; holidays vary and are a known limitation. |
| 28 | Investor governance is a per-company tracker seeded from the deck's requirements. No consent thresholds are hard-coded; company-specific requirements are added per company. | The deck's thresholds are illustrative. |
| 29 | Priority focus: `settings.priority_target` (default 3) drives warnings; `settings.max_priorities` (default 5) is a database hard limit excluding stopped priorities. | "About three" is guidance, not a rule; a ceiling stops sprawl. |
| 30 | Section 15 of the v4 update was truncated ("Omni OS is not intended to replace: accounting systems, HR information…"). It is read as: Omni OS does not replace accounting, HRIS, CRM or similar systems of record. | Text ended mid-list. |
| 31 | The annual strategy reset is a quarterly offsite flagged `is_annual`, adding a stage for next year's 12-month objectives and budget assumptions. | One workflow, as the deck describes the annual reset as one of the quarterly offsites. |
| 32 | Agents are deterministic playbooks on a Postgres-backed step machine, not an agent framework or an LLM loop. Each step is checkpointed; side effects are idempotent by key. | Auditable, restart-safe and testable on real data; the brief asked not to replace the architecture with a framework. |
| 33 | Each company has its own agent principal (a banned-login auth user with membership role `agent`) and the worker mints short-lived JWTs for it, so agents go through the same RLS as people. The role is read-only on management records; consequential writes are security-definer functions that re-check authority or a specific approved proposal at execution. | Tenant isolation and authority enforced in the database, not in agent code. |
| 34 | Level 5 categories are capped at *approval* in the database; `commercial_terms` is capped at *off*. New companies start with all agents off. | Conservative by default; no configuration can grant unrestricted authority. |
| 35 | Proposals are immutable (payload hash); approval authorises exactly one action; the approver's role and the record's preconditions are re-checked before execution, otherwise the proposal goes *stale*. | Approval must mean exactly what was shown. |
| 36 | External acquisition approaches are never sent by an agent: approval creates a Mission Control action for the deal lead with the approved draft. | No agent commits a company externally; a person sends it. |
| 37 | The Omni OS inbox is the default channel; email/Outlook are optional per-company connections with a sandbox mode. No external messages are sent from this environment. | No provider credentials; nothing pretends to send. |
| 38 | Recovery and pipeline changes after an intervention are recorded, never attributed to the agent without further evidence; outcome measures separate activity from results. | Avoids overstating impact. |
| 39 | HubSpot, Companies House, Resend and Graph connectors use official APIs but are untested without credentials; OAuth is not built (admins store an access token). Sandbox CRM and market datasets are fictional and flagged. | Honest scope. |
